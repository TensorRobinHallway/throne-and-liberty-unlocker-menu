## 🧩 About

**Throne and Liberty catalog Menu** is a curated list of Throne and Liberty open-source tools:

- **Overlays** — on-screen helpers, layout notes, map viewer, waypoint notes
- **Quality-of-Life Helpers** — loot catalog, gear manager, overlay notes
- **Research Notes** — exploration layouts and config notes for educational use

Based on publicly available community tools and overlay helpers.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Adventure Helpers
- Waypoint Notes — mark and review travel routes
- Map Viewer — visualize zone layouts and points of interest
- Route Notes — record and review exploration runs
- Travel Speed Notes — adjust map playback for study (0.1x to 5x)

### 🎨 Catalog & Cosmetics
- Loot Catalog — browse drops, sets, and appearance items
- Gear Notes — organize loadouts and cosmetics
- Map Overlay — improved zone visibility

### 🏋️ Exploration Layout
- Zone Notes — segment study on any region
- Camera Notes — manually review angles in the editor
- Combo Notes — chain complex maneuvers in the planner
